Design notes

One gesture.
Every surface considered.

Four decisions shape Echo. Each one removes something rather than adding it.

Open, then closed

Two hemispherical cups and a retracting band meet to form a sphere. One guided gesture, no twisting or detachable parts.

Twin-rail headband

The band descends on two rails and seats flush into the crown. It never rotates, so the geometry stays predictable.

Bio-Energy fit

Weight and contact are mapped across the crown and temples before the shell is shaped. Comfort is a design input.

Built to be kept

Cushions and wear parts are replaceable. The shell can be opened and serviced rather than sealed for disposal.

Questions

Before you decide.

What is Evaion Echo?

Echo is a closed-back, over-ear headphone whose hemispherical cups and retractable band close into a single sphere. It is sold in one finish, Violet Alloy.

How does the mechanism work?

A twin-rail band descends into the crown while the cups glide together along the centre axis. The cushions tuck behind the rim and the seam closes. You can see the sequence on the homepage.

Is Echo wireless?

Yes. Echo uses Bluetooth 5.3 with multipoint pairing, and USB-C for wired listening and charging.

How long does the battery last?

Up to 40 hours of playback. A full charge takes about two hours; ten minutes gives roughly four hours.

What does the warranty cover?

Two years from delivery for defects in materials or workmanship. Wear parts and accidental damage are not covered. Read the warranty page.

Can I return Echo?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery if it is unused and complete in the original packaging. Read shipping and returns.

What is Bio-Energy Technology?

Evaion's design framework for the relationship between body, material, sound and space. It is not a battery, medical device or energy-harvesting claim.
