Order processing

Orders placed before 14:00 IST on a business day are usually packed the same day. Orders placed after that, or on weekends and public holidays, are packed on the next business day.

You will receive a confirmation email when the order is placed and a dispatch email with tracking when Echo leaves the warehouse.

Tracking

The dispatch email includes a tracking link and the courier's name. If tracking has not updated for 48 hours, contact care@evaion.in with your order number and we will investigate.

Returns

Echo can be returned within 14 days of delivery if it is unused, complete and in the original packaging. A return is inspected before a refund is issued.

  1. Email care@evaion.in with your order number and reason for return.
  2. We confirm the return and arrange a pickup where available.
  3. Pack Echo in its case with all accessories and documentation.
  4. After inspection, the refund is issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Return shipping is free for damaged or incorrect items. For change-of-mind returns, a pickup charge may apply.

Opened, used or incomplete products cannot be returned unless they are faulty. Warranty claims are handled separately.
