Two hemispherical cups, one band that lowers on twin rails and seats flush into the crown. No twisting, no detachable parts.

  1. 01

    Hemispherical cups

    Each cup is half of the closed sphere. Shell, cushion and driver are arranged so the two halves meet without a visible gap.

  2. 02

    Twin rails

    The headband descends on two parallel rails. It never rotates or detaches, so the closed shape is repeatable every time.

  3. 03

    One seam

    Closed, a single centre seam and a small status light remain. Nothing else moves for effect.
