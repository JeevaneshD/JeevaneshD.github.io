Evaion / Design principle 01

How we work

Three rules.
No exceptions.

01

Begin with the body

Weight, contact and temperature come before specifications. If an object is not comfortable, it is not finished.

02

Make it repairable

Wear parts should be replaceable and the shell should open. Longevity is an engineering decision, not a slogan.

03

Say only what is true

No invented certifications and no vague technology claims. Every number on this site is sample copy for the demonstration.

The studio

Close to
the work.

Evaion is a small team of designers and engineers working between industrial design, acoustics and software. We prototype in the same room where we listen, which keeps the feedback loop short and the decisions honest.

Echo began as a study of how a headphone could disappear when it is not being worn. The answer was not a smaller case. It was a different geometry.
