Care & charging

A few habits.
A long life.

How should I clean Echo?

Wipe the shell with a soft, dry cloth. Clean the cushions with a damp cloth and mild soap, then let them air-dry. Do not soak Echo or use solvents, alcohol or abrasives.

How long does charging take?

A full charge takes about two hours over USB-C. Ten minutes of charging gives roughly four hours of playback. Use the supplied cable with a certified adapter.

Can I replace the cushions?

Yes. Cushions are replaceable wear parts. Contact support with your order number and we will send the correct pair with fitting instructions.

How should Echo be stored?

Close Echo into the sphere and place it in the travel case. Keep it away from heat, moisture and prolonged direct sunlight.

Warranty & service

Repair, not
replace.

What does the warranty cover?

Two years from delivery for defects in materials or workmanship under normal use. The full terms, exclusions and claim process are on the warranty page.

How do I start a claim?

Email care@evaion.in with your order number, a description of the issue and photographs if possible. We reply within one business day with the next step.

What is not covered?

Accidental damage, liquid damage, normal wear of cushions, and damage from unauthorised repair or modification. Consumable wear parts can be purchased separately.

How long does a repair take?

Most repairs are completed within seven to ten business days after Echo reaches our service centre. We will confirm the timeline before you send it.

Where is my order?

Tracking is emailed when Echo is collected by the courier. Standard delivery takes two to five business days across India; remote pin codes can take up to eight.

Can I change my address after ordering?

Email us before dispatch and we will update the address if the parcel has not left the warehouse. After dispatch, the courier may be able to redirect the parcel.

Do you ship outside India?

Not at launch. Echo is currently available in India only. International availability will be listed on this page when confirmed.

Is my payment secure?

In production, payment is collected by a hosted provider. Evaion does not receive or store full card numbers. This demonstration never processes a payment.
